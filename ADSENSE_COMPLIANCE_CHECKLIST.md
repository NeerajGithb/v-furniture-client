## 🔍 Before Requesting AdSense Review

1. **Test All Pages**
   - Visit each policy page and verify content displays correctly
   - Check footer links work on all pages
   - Test on both desktop and mobile

2. **Content Check**
   - At least 20-30 high-quality product listings
   - Unique product descriptions (minimum 150-200 words each)
   - Clear, professional images
   - No duplicate content

3. **Navigation**
   - Easy to navigate between pages
   - Clear menu structure
   - Breadcrumbs on product pages
   - Working search

4. **Contact Information**
   - Visible on every page (footer)
   - Working contact form
   - Valid email address
   - Phone number (if applicable)

5. **Professional Appearance**
   - Consistent design across all pages
   - No broken layouts
   - Professional color scheme
   - Readable fonts

## 📝 AdSense Application Tips

1. **Wait for Traffic**: Have at least 50-100 daily visitors before applying
2. **Original Content**: Ensure all content is unique and valuable
3. **Active Site**: Update products/content regularly
4. **Clean Design**: Professional, easy-to-navigate layout
5. **No Policy Violations**: Review AdSense policies carefully
